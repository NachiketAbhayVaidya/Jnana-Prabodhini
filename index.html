About Jnana Prabodhini

Jnana Prabodhini is a renowned organization dedicated to identifying and nurturing promising young minds who demonstrate intellectual, social and leadership potential. With initiatives in education, research, social development, and national awareness, it inspires youth toward nation-building and meaningful contribution to society.

Know More About The Speaker

Dr. Siddharth Dhomkar is a distinguished faculty member at the Department of Physics, IIT Madras, and an Honorary Research Associate at University College London. His work spans cutting-edge research in quantum physics, quantum computing, and experimental quantum technologies. He has been actively involved in simplifying complex scientific concepts for students and enthusiasts, with the mission of promoting scientific curiosity and accessible learning.
